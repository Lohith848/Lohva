Process

How It Works

01 — WAKE
Say "Nova"
CYRUS runs silently in your system tray. Say the wake word and the Arc Reactor HUD appears on screen immediately.
nova
02 — COMMAND
Speak Your Command
Say any command naturally. CYRUS uses Vosk for fully offline speech recognition — your voice never leaves your PC.
open chrome  /  weather in chennai
03 — EXECUTE
CYRUS Acts
The command is instantly routed to the correct handler — system control, AI reasoning, messaging, or web search.
04 — REPLY
Speaks Back and Closes
CYRUS responds using a clear voice, then the HUD hides automatically. Ready for the next wake word.
